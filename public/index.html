About

Building secure systems and strong teams.

I'm a Technical Lead Manager at General Motors in Austin, Texas. Over 13+ years at GM I've grown from software developer to staff engineer in Product Cybersecurity, and now I lead the engineering teams behind it. My work sits where large-scale distributed systems meet security: vehicle unlock and verification, cryptographic signing, and the APIs that tie it all together.

Before GM I spent twenty years freelancing — fixing computers, wiring networks and building websites for local businesses around Houston. That's where I learned that good technology is about helping people.
