About

Engineer by trade, people-person by nature.

I've spent more than a decade at General Motors, growing from software developer to staff engineer in Product Cybersecurity and now to Technical Lead Manager. My work sits where large-scale distributed systems meet security: vehicle unlock and verification, cryptographic signing, and the APIs that tie it all together.

Before GM I spent twenty years freelancing — fixing computers, wiring networks and building websites for local businesses around Houston. That's where I learned that great technology is really about helping people.
